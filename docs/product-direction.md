# Product direction

The synthetic demo verifies the data flow but does not answer a real research question. The next iteration should deliver three connected outcomes:

1. **Understand a company:** a concise overview of its business, report periods, and major risk topics, linked to original disclosures.
2. **Understand changes:** compare real annual filings, group evidence by topic, distinguish added disclosures from rewording or reordering, and show surrounding context. Avoid claiming a risk is more severe simply because text grew.
3. **Follow a watchlist:** save tickers and surface new annual reports and review-worthy changes, with source links and filing dates. Start with on-demand refresh; scheduled monitoring can follow.

## First useful slice

Use Apple 2024/2025 as a verified extraction benchmark. Present Business and Risk Factors from actual reports, establish accurate boundaries and topic grouping, and then generalize to ticker lookup. Add a watchlist only after a single-company comparison reliably answers “what changed, and where is the evidence?”

The public demo must remain labeled synthetic until real evidence is available. Backend hosting alone does not make the demo useful or establish live SEC access.
